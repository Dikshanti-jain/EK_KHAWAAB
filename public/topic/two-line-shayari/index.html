Two-Line Shayari (2-Line Sher) — Maximum Meaning in Minimum Words

A couplet consists of just two lines: Misra-e-Ula (opening line) and Misra-e-Sani (closing punchline).

A sher is the ultimate masterstroke of Urdu literature. In just two metered lines, a poet captures an ocean of philosophy, romantic longing, or quiet defiance. Explore our collection across all three scripts.

by @Dil_e_Beqasoor • Sher • Heartbreak & Hijr

Ab kisi baat pe dil dukhta nahi hai mera, / Tumne is hadd tak sataya hai ki patthar kar diya.
