Vehshat (वहशत • وحشت) — Meaning, Pronunciation & Poetic Usage

Pronunciation: /veh-shat/ • Origin: Urdu • English Meaning: Wild restlessness, bewildered frenzy of the heart

Literary Context & Classical Usage

A profound frenzy born of overwhelming sorrow or beauty.

"Vehshat mein bhi ek tarteeb rakhi humne"

Related Poetic Words in Lughat
